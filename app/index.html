Read this first: one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the list's only possibly missable trophy. The first meditation is Lucia's balcony session at Jason's house in Chapter 1, offered after the mission Down to Earth — do it before Chapter 1 ends. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, KeenGamer.
